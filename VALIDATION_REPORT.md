## 8. Timing and gap semantics — verified empirically

* `test_signal_layer_applies_no_shift` — signals carry `decision_timestamp = close_time + 1ms`, unshifted.
* `test_execution_happens_at_next_bar_open` / `test_no_double_shift` — exactly one shift.
* `test_no_execution_across_gap` — no entry or exit inside a declared gap.
* `test_gap_boundary_exit_recorded` — open position exits at last valid close, reason `GAP_BOUNDARY`.
* **NEW `test_stale_signal_is_not_executed_after_a_gap`** — a signal on the last bar before a
  gap expires; it is never carried across and filled at the first post-gap bar.
* **NEW `test_execution_index_requires_the_true_next_minute`** — engine-level proof that the
  execution candle must be the true next minute, otherwise the intent is dropped.
* Feature windows restart after a gap (`test_feature_window_invalid_after_gap`), and MTF
  candles overlapping a gap are invalidated (`test_gap_candles_invalidated`).

Late execution of a stale signal after a gap is therefore **not** supported — by design and by test.

* **Everything about real BTCUSDT data**: hash, rows, date range, gap registry
  consistency, real-data backtest/walk-forward/Monte Carlo. Untested here.
* No paper-operation session against a live feed; no reconciliation loop against a real venue.

  authenticated client) — therefore unreviewed and untested.

  features inside Genome conditions remain unsupported in v1.0.
* Synthetic-data metrics in §4–§6 are pipeline evidence only, never strategy evidence.
